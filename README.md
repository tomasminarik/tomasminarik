### How I work

- **Good design helps people and elevates businesses.** User needs shouldn't back down to business needs, and both can be met at the same time.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Ambiguity is part of the job; the known part is decided on facts and insights.
- **Shipping over talking.** No UX theatre. Learn, build, ship and repeat.
